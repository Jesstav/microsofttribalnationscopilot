Copilot Enablement Webinar Series

Copilot in Action: Role-Based Enablement for Every Team

January 19-20, 2027 · 11 live sessions · Virtual · All times Central (CT)

Copilot works best when it fits the work your team already does. Over two days, this series shows Tribal government and enterprise teams how Microsoft 365 Copilot can help them draft, summarize, analyze and catch up faster in the apps they use every day, while keeping sensitive information protected.

Each one-hour session focuses on a single role, such as HR, Finance, IT, Legal, Operations, Executives or Administrative Professionals, and uses practical examples your team can try right away. If you're planning a rollout, join Driving Copilot Adoption for a step-by-step playbook.
